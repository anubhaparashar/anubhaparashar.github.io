Energy Consumption Optimization for Smart Classroom Using Computer Vision

IoT Project · Manipal University Jaipur July 2019 – December 2019 ₹4,00,000 INR

A research project aimed at reducing unnecessary energy consumption in smart homes, offices, and classrooms using wireless sensor networks (WSNs) and computer vision. An isolated WSN with one coordinator integrated into Arduino Uno was deployed per room to transfer environmental parameters to the management station.

Passive infrared sensors were used to automatically control lighting — turning lights on when a person is detected and off after a predefined delay. Analysis of fluorescent lamp illumination alongside PIR sensors demonstrated significant energy savings.
