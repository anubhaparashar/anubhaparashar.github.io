Leadership & Responsibilities

01
Vice Captain, School
2007 · Class 10
St. George's Girls Grammar School
Elected as Vice Captain of the school, representing the student body at the highest level of student governance. Responsibilities included supporting school-level discipline, coordinating inter-house events and cultural activities, liaising between students and school administration, and upholding the school's values of integrity and service. The role was formalised at the annual Investiture Ceremony — a formal occasion where student leaders take a public oath of commitment to the school and its community.

Assisted in maintaining discipline, mentoring junior students, and coordinating student activities.
