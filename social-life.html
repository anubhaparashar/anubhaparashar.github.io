Connecting Beyond Research: Community Engagement & Professional Networking

Life as a researcher is rich with connections — from spirited debates at international conferences to late-evening discussions at local AI meetups. These moments of human connection are as vital to my work as any experiment or paper.

In May 2025, I had the privilege of hosting a session at the Global AI Jaipur Meetup — a vibrant gathering of AI practitioners, students, and enthusiasts from across Rajasthan and beyond. Events like these are where abstract research meets real-world curiosity. Sharing insights on deep learning applications and hearing fresh perspectives from attendees always reignites my enthusiasm for the field.

Beyond formal conferences, I believe strongly in nurturing an academic community that is welcoming and collaborative. Whether it is mentoring junior researchers, participating in faculty development workshops, or simply being present at departmental seminars, the social fabric of academia is what transforms individual effort into collective progress.

"The best ideas often emerge not in solitude, but in the conversations that happen between sessions at a conference — over chai, at a whiteboard, or walking back to the hotel."

Professionally, the value of networking cannot be overstated. Collaborations on grants, co-authored papers, and interdisciplinary projects have all grown from connections made at events, workshops, and online communities. I am grateful for every researcher, student, and practitioner who has taken the time to engage, question, and inspire.

If you are an AI or tech enthusiast in the Jaipur region — or anywhere in the world — I warmly invite you to connect, attend events, and join the growing community of curious minds pushing the boundaries of intelligent technology.

01Happy Birthday to Our Beautiful Mom ♥

May 19, 2026 Family & Blessings 16 Photos
Our beautiful Mom Love you forever

Today is not just another day. Today is the birthday of the most beautiful woman in our lives — our mother, our strength, our blessing, our first home, and our forever safe place.

Mom, when we look at you, we see beauty in its purest form. Not only the beauty of your face, your smile, and your presence, but the beauty of your heart, your soul, your patience, your sacrifices, and your unconditional love. You have given so much of yourself to us that no words will ever be enough to thank you. You have loved us silently, supported us endlessly, and stood by us through every season of life.

You are the person who has always been there — not only in our happy moments, but also in our struggles, fears, confusion, failures, and nervous days. Whenever life felt difficult, your presence made it lighter. Whenever we felt weak, your blessings made us stronger. Whenever we doubted ourselves, your belief in us reminded us that we could do it.

For me, you have been present in every important milestone of my life. From my very first presentation, to my PhD presentation, to my company presentation — you were always there. You stood beside me like my biggest support system, encouraging me, blessing me, and making sure I never felt alone. In every hurdle, every nervous moment, and every difficult day, your presence gave me strength. You may not always say much, but your silent support has always spoken the loudest.

One memory that will always stay close to my heart is how you looked after Sam for eight long hours in a room while I completed my company presentation. You never complained. You never made me feel guilty. You never made me feel like I was asking too much. You simply stood by me, like you always do — with love, patience, strength, and a smile.

I truly believe that the luckiest people are those who can take their mother with them to the most important milestones of their life. Lucky are those who can have their mother sitting beside them at their first presentation, standing proudly with them at their PhD presentation, and even being present at their company presentation. I am one of those blessed people. I can take you everywhere with me — in my achievements, in my struggles, in my journey, in my motherhood, and in every new chapter of my life.

And this birthday wish is from both of us — from me and Apoo, your two daughters, who love you more than words can ever express. Apoo is not just a part of this bond; she is one whole heart of it. You, Apoo, and I share a connection that is so pure, so deep, and so precious that it cannot be explained in simple words. We three have grown together through every phase of life — sharing love, laughter, worries, dreams, small fights, big emotions, silent understanding, and endless memories.

Mom, you have held both of us with the same love, protected both of us with the same strength, and blessed both of us with the same heart. Apoo and I may be different in many ways, but when it comes to you, our love is the same — deep, emotional, and unconditional. You are the center of our world, and together the three of us are not just mother and daughters; we are each other’s comfort, strength, and home.

From Apoo’s heart too, this message is for you: Mom, you are our biggest blessing. Your love has shaped us, your sacrifices have protected us, and your prayers have guided us. We may not always say it perfectly, but we feel it every day — life is better, safer, and more beautiful because we have you. Thank you for loving us so deeply, for standing by us so strongly, and for being the most beautiful mother we could ever ask for.

And even today, your love and support continue in the most beautiful way. Till date, Sam is being looked after with so much care, love, and patience by only you and Papa. The way both of you take care of him, protect him, love him, and make sure he never feels alone is something I can never repay in this lifetime.

For a mother, seeing her own parents take care of her child with the same love and warmth is the biggest blessing. Sam is growing under your love, your values, your affection, and your blessings. Whatever I am able to do today — my work, my responsibilities, my dreams, and my journey — it is possible because I know Sam is safe, loved, and cared for by you and Papa.

You and Papa have become his first home, his comfort, and his biggest support. The love you both give him is priceless. I feel blessed not only because you are my mother, but because Sam also has you and Papa as his strongest protectors and most loving grandparents.

Mom, your love did not stop with me. It continues through Sam. And that makes me even more grateful, even more emotional, and even more proud to be your daughter.

This era of our life is truly the best because we have you with us. We are living our dreams, building our lives, facing our responsibilities, raising our family, and growing every day — and through it all, you are there. Your presence makes everything more meaningful. Our success feels happier because you are there to witness it. Our struggles feel easier because you are there to hold us. Our journey feels complete because you are walking beside us.

Whatever I am today is because of you. Whatever we are today is because of you. Every achievement carries your prayers. Every milestone carries your blessings. Every success carries your sacrifices. Behind our confidence is your encouragement. Behind our courage is your strength. Behind our smiles is your love. Behind everything we have become, there is you.

You have taught us what true love looks like. You have taught us what sacrifice means. You have taught us how to be strong without becoming hard, how to care without expecting anything, and how to keep giving even when life is not easy. You are not just our mother — you are our inspiration, our guide, our protector, our blessing, and the reason we believe in ourselves.

Sometimes “thank you” feels too small for everything you have done. How can we thank someone who gave us life, raised us with love, stood by us in every difficulty, supported our dreams, cared for our family, and still continues to give without asking for anything in return?

Mom, you are our greatest blessing. We feel proud to be your daughters. We feel lucky to have your hand on our heads, your prayers in our lives, and your presence in our journey. No matter how much we achieve in life, our biggest blessing will always be having a mother like you.

On your birthday, we only pray that God gives you all the happiness, peace, love, health, and comfort that you truly deserve. May your life be filled with smiles, good health, beautiful memories, and endless love. You have spent your life making others happy, and now we wish that every day brings happiness back to you.

Happy Birthday, Mom.
We love you more than words can ever express.
Whatever we are today, and whatever we will become tomorrow, it will always be because of you. ♥

Love you forever, Mom.
From both your daughters, with all our love — and with heartfelt love and respect from Ashu. ♥
